Social Media-Worthy Events in Chandigarh — Design Tips from VR Productions

Jun 24, 2024 • 6 min read • By VR Productions

In 2025, a successful event isn't just experienced by those present — it's shared with thousands of followers on Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube. Creating social media-worthy events requires deliberate design choices that look spectacular on camera.

Essential Social Media-Worthy Event Elements

Instagrammable backdrops: Every event needs at least one stunning visual focal point — a floral wall, neon sign, or sculptural installation — that guests will naturally gravitate toward for photos.

Good lighting: Ring lights, golden fairy lights, and professional event lighting make the difference between dark, blurry phone photos and stunning images.

Event hashtag: Create a custom event hashtag and display it prominently so all guest photos are aggregated in one place.

Photo Booth Design for Events in Chandigarh

Custom photo booths with branded backdrops, prop sets, and professional printers allow guests to take home physical memories while sharing digital versions online.

Colour Palette Consistency

Events with a cohesive color palette photograph dramatically better than cluttered multi-color setups — and the photos look beautiful displayed together on Instagram.
