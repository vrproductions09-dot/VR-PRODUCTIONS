Top Wedding Décor Trends for 2025 in Chandigarh & Tricity

Jan 20, 2025 • 7 min read • By VR Productions

Wedding décor trends change every year, and 2025 is bringing some truly exciting directions for Tricity weddings. As one of Chandigarh's leading event designers, VR Productions shares the trends we're seeing in our bookings.

1. Moody Floral Palettes

Dark, romantic floral arrangements featuring burgundy, deep purple, black calla lilies, and forest green foliage are replacing the pastel palettes of recent years.

2. Sustainable & Local Flowers

Eco-conscious couples are requesting locally sourced seasonal flowers — marigolds, roses, and jasmine — arranged in innovative ways that reduce environmental impact.

3. Maximalist Ceiling Installations

Dramatic overhead floral clouds, chandelier clusters, and draped fabric ceilings that transform bland banquet halls into jaw-dropping spaces.

4. Personalized Neon Signs

Custom neon signs with couple names, wedding dates, and personal phrases are appearing at every wedding photoshoot spot in 2025.

5. Micro-Wedding Luxury

Smaller guest lists with higher per-head spending, creating more intimate but ultra-luxurious experiences — a growing trend in Chandigarh.
