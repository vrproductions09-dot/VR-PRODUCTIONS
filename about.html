Who We Are

Born in Chandigarh,
Built for Tricity

VR Productions was founded in Chandigarh with a single belief — every event deserves to be extraordinary. What started as a small team of passionate event enthusiasts has grown into Tricity's most sought-after event management company.

Today, we manage over 100 events a year across Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula — from intimate weddings of 50 guests to grand concerts of 5000. Every event gets the same obsessive attention to detail.

Our clients trust us because we treat their events like our own. We don't just execute — we create memories.
