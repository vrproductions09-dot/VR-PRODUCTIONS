Tricity Event Industry in 2025 — Trends & Insights from VR Productions

May 20, 2024 • 6 min read • By VR Productions

The event industry in Tricity has undergone remarkable transformation over the past few years. As Chandigarh, Mohali, and Panchkula continue to grow as economic and cultural hubs, the demand for sophisticated event management has grown correspondingly.

Key Trends in Tricity Events 2025

Micro-luxury: Smaller guest lists with higher per-head investment creating more intimate, luxurious experiences.
Sustainability: Eco-conscious clients requesting local flowers, reduced plastic, and minimal waste events.
Technology integration: LED walls, projection mapping, and social media live streaming becoming standard.
Experiential design: Events designed around guest experiences rather than just aesthetics.

Growth of Corporate Events in Tricity

With IT companies and startups proliferating in Mohali's IT City, corporate events have seen 40% year-on-year growth in Tricity. VR Productions has been the preferred corporate event partner for many of these organizations.
