10 Tips for Planning a Perfect Indian Wedding in Tricity

Apr 28, 2025 • 8 min read • By VR Productions

Indian weddings are elaborate, multi-day affairs that require months of planning and dozens of vendors. Here are 10 expert tips from VR Productions — Tricity's leading wedding planners.

1. Start Planning at Least 6 Months Early

Top venues in Chandigarh book out 8-12 months in advance, especially during peak season (November to February). Start your planning journey as early as possible.

2. Set a Realistic Budget

Break your budget into clear categories: venue (30%), décor (25%), catering (25%), entertainment (10%), and miscellaneous (10%). VR Productions provides transparent cost breakdowns upfront.

3. Choose the Right Wedding Planner

Look for planners with 5+ years of local experience, strong vendor relationships, and verifiable client reviews. VR Productions has 8+ years in Tricity with 500+ successful events.

4. Lock in Your Key Vendors Early

Photographer, caterer, decorator, and DJ — these four get booked fastest. We recommend finalizing these at least 4 months in advance.

5. Plan for Tricity Weather

Winters (Nov-Feb) are perfect but cold evenings require heating arrangements. Summers need air-cooled venues or outdoor setups with coolers. Monsoon requires covered venues.

6. Coordinate All Functions Together

From roka to reception, having one planner coordinate all functions ensures consistent aesthetics, vendor coordination, and cost savings. VR Productions specializes in full-wedding packages.

7. Personalize Your Décor

Move beyond generic mandap setups. Work with your planner to create a unique theme that reflects your personalities — floral, royal, bohemian, or contemporary.

8. Guest Management Matters

Tricity weddings often have 300-1500 guests. Professional guest management — RSVPs, seating, transportation, accommodation coordination — is essential and often overlooked.

9. Don't Skimp on Entertainment

Entertainment makes memories. Live music, sangeet performances, LED dance floors, and celebrity artists elevate your wedding from good to unforgettable.

10. Have a Backup Plan

Always plan for contingencies — weather changes, vendor delays, power issues. VR Productions maintains backup vendor lists and on-site generator arrangements for all events.
