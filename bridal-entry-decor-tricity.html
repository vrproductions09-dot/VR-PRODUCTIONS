Our Bridal Entry Styles

Bridal Entry Decor Options in Tricity

Every bride is unique. We offer a wide range of entry styles — choose one or combine multiple for a truly unforgettable entrance.

🌸

Flower Shower Entry

A cascade of fresh petals raining down as the bride walks in — rose petals, marigold, or mixed florals creating a dreamy, Instagram-perfect moment.

🌫️

Fog Tunnel / Dry Ice Entry

A low-lying fog carpet created with dry ice that makes the bride appear to float on clouds. One of the most dramatic and popular bridal entry styles in Tricity.

✨

LED Petal Path

A glowing pathway of LED lights and fresh flower petals guiding the bride from the entrance to the mandap — elegant and timeless.

🎆

Cold Pyro Entry

Sparkling cold pyrotechnic fountains lining the bridal path — safe, spectacular and perfect for indoor venues in Chandigarh and Mohali.

🎊

Confetti Cannon Entry

Bursts of golden or coloured confetti as the bride makes her entrance — joyful, celebratory and guaranteed to get the crowd cheering.

🏵️

Floral Arch Entry

A stunning floral archway framing the bride's entrance — custom designed with roses, orchids, or marigolds to match your wedding colour palette.

💡

Fairy Light Canopy Entry

A canopy of thousands of fairy lights creating a starlit overhead effect as the bride walks beneath — magical for evening weddings.

🪞

Mirror / LED Backdrop Entry

Custom LED panels or mirror walls creating a dazzling backdrop behind the bride for photography and video — makes every shot stunning.

🎵

Live Dhol + Dancer Entry

Traditional Punjabi dhol players and dancers accompanying the bride for a high-energy, joyful entry that gets every guest on their feet.
