Outdoor Event Planning in Chandigarh — Tips & Venues

Sep 9, 2024 • 6 min read • By VR Productions

Chandigarh's beautiful winter weather (October to February) makes it one of North India's finest outdoor event destinations. With wide open spaces, beautiful parks, and numerous farmhouse properties, outdoor events in Chandigarh can be truly spectacular.

Best Outdoor Event Locations in Chandigarh

Sector 17 Plaza for public events, Leisure Valley for smaller gatherings, Rock Garden amphitheatre for performances, and numerous private farmhouses in Zirakpur, Kharar, and Derabassi for private events.

Weather Management for Outdoor Events

Winter evenings in Chandigarh can get cold (below 10°C). VR Productions always includes heating arrangements, guest comfort planning, and covered contingency setups for outdoor events.

Power & Infrastructure for Outdoor Events

Outdoor events require independent power supply, temporary toilet facilities, adequate parking coordination, and security arrangements — all of which VR Productions manages comprehensively.
