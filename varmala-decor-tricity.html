Varmala Decor Tricity

The Varmala Moment Deserves a Stunning Stage

The varmala — the exchange of garlands between the bride and groom — is the heart of every Hindu and Punjabi wedding. Every eye in the room is watching. Every camera is pointed at that stage. VR Productions ensures this moment looks as beautiful as it feels.

We design and execute varmala stage setups, jaimala stands, backdrop arrangements and special effects specifically for this ceremony — serving weddings across Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula and all of Tricity for over 10 years.

Varmala Decor Styles

Our Varmala Stage & Decor Options

🌺

Floral Jaimala Stand

Custom jaimala stands crafted with fresh roses, marigolds, orchids or mixed florals — designed to match your wedding colour palette and theme perfectly.

🎆

Cold Pyro Varmala Blast

The moment the garlands are exchanged, cold pyro fountains on both sides of the stage fire in a spectacular burst — the most viral varmala moment in Tricity.

🌸

Confetti & Petal Shower

Cannons releasing rose petals or golden confetti the moment the varmala is placed — magical, joyful and absolutely stunning on camera.

💡

LED Backdrop Stage

A custom LED video wall or fairy light backdrop behind the couple during the varmala — creates a glowing, cinematic frame for every photograph.

🏵️

Luxury Floral Stage Setup

Full floral stage decoration with mandap-style arrangement for the varmala area — roses, tuberoses, jasmine and marigold in your chosen colours.

🎊

Ribbon Pull Surprise

A ribbon-pull mechanism releases a shower of petals or balloons over the couple at the varmala moment — a delightful surprise the couple and guests love.

🕯️

Candlelight Varmala Setup

An intimate candlelit stage arrangement for evening weddings — warm, romantic and incredibly photogenic for the varmala ceremony.

🎵

Live Dhol at Varmala

Live dhol players performing at the exact moment of the varmala exchange — traditional, high-energy and loved by Punjabi families across Tricity.

🌫️

Fog Stage Effect

Low-lying fog on the stage floor during the varmala creating a dreamy, cloud-like effect — beautiful for evening or indoor weddings in Chandigarh.
